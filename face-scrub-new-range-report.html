Overview

Why Nat Habit is exploring a new face scrub range, and what this report is built from.

About this report. Nat Habit's current face scrub, Mura, has a ~2-month shelf life and needs refrigeration — a format that is hard to manufacture and ship, and (per our own existing-user research) suppresses the habit it's meant to build. This report combines three studies to build the case for a new, shelf-stable face scrub range: a 59-respondent consumer survey on face scrub attitudes and needs (mixed Nat Habit users and non-users), a 50-respondent cohort study of existing Mura users (Active Loyalists, Active Trialists, Active Users, Lapsers and Loyal Lapsers), and a market landscaping study of 75 competing face-scrub ASINs across Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, Myntra and quick-commerce (Jan 2026).
